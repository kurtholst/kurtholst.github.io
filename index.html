About me

Bridging enterprise data and modern AI

I'm a Solution Architect at Databricks, helping organizations turn ambitious data & AI ideas into working, production-grade solutions on the Lakehouse. My focus is end-to-end: data ingestion and governance, machine learning and GenAI, natural-language analytics with Genie, and the apps that put it all in front of business users.

My background runs deep in enterprise data – years of hands-on work with SAP HANA, machine learning in R and Python, and Spark – which lets me speak both the language of established enterprise systems and the modern Lakehouse. I like building the whole thing: from synthetic data generators and declarative pipelines to model-serving endpoints and full-stack Databricks Apps.

Lately I've been shipping GenAI-powered prototypes – predictive-maintenance agents, maintenance control towers, and end-to-end industry data workbenches – often built entirely through natural-language prompts. I care about solutions that actually run, with the evidence to prove it.
