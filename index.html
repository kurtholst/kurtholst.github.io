About me

Bridging enterprise data and modern AI

I'm a Solutions Architect at Databricks, helping organizations turn ambitious data & AI ideas into working, production-grade solutions on the Lakehouse. My focus is end-to-end: data ingestion and governance, machine learning and GenAI, natural-language analytics with Genie, and the apps that put it all in front of business users.

My background runs deep - 25 years in data & analytics, from building Business Intelligence and data warehouses in the late 1990s, through years as a Principal Consultant in SAP Predictive Analytics and SAP HANA, to data science with machine learning and geospatial analytics. That history lets me speak both the language of established enterprise systems and the modern Lakehouse. I like building the whole thing: from synthetic data generators and declarative pipelines to model-serving endpoints and full-stack Databricks Apps.

For four years I also taught as an external lecturer at the University of Southern Denmark - every Friday, delivering "Data Mining & Data Warehousing" and counselling bachelor's and master's thesis students on Business Intelligence, Data Warehousing, and the Balanced Scorecard. Explaining complex ideas simply is still central to how I work: several former students credit that teaching in the recommendations below.

Lately I've been shipping GenAI-powered prototypes – predictive-maintenance agents, maintenance control towers, and end-to-end industry data workbenches – often built entirely through natural-language prompts. I care about solutions that actually run, with the evidence to prove it.

Above all I bring an AI-first, builder, tech-first mindset: I reach for the newest AI and platform capabilities early, and I'd rather prove an idea by building a working prototype than debate it in slides. I stay hands-on with the tech - writing the code, wiring the pipelines, and shipping the app - so that recommendations are grounded in what actually works, not just what sounds good.

Featured case study

An agentic AI system that boosts Overall Equipment Effectiveness

Problem

Manufacturers lose hidden capacity to downtime, speed loss, and defects - but OEE data is scattered across shifts and sites, and root-cause knowledge is locked in PDFs. Teams can't ask questions in plain language or get explained, actionable answers.

Approach

A multi-agent architecture on Databricks: Genie for governed conversational SQL over Delta OEE marts, an Agent Bricks Knowledge Assistant (RAG) over root-cause PDFs, an MCP web-search connector, and a Mosaic AI supervisor routing each question to the right tool - all traced with MLflow, surfaced in a Databricks App.

Outcome

Operators get consistent, cited OEE answers and "what-if" capacity estimates in seconds instead of chasing spreadsheets. Published as a reproducible reference architecture - and awarded 2nd place at the Databricks Hackathon (Dec 2025).

Kunstig intelligens til kortlægning af særlig værdifuld skov

"AI for mapping particularly valuable forest (§25)"
Danish Environmental Protection Agency

A supervised CatBoost classifier that predicts where naturally valuable forest occurs in private woodland, so costly field surveys can be targeted. Fuses LiDAR point clouds, Sentinel-2 optical bands, orthophotos, soil/terrain maps, and local weather into a 10×10 m probability map - trained and scaled on Databricks with stratified cluster sampling to avoid geographic bias.
